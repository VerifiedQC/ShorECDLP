## Figure 15 and Figure 14 schedules

The pinned in-place division schedule is exactly:

1. forward EEA on `X, A, S`;
2. compute `X*Y` into `A`;
3. X-basis measure and reset the old `Y`;
4. inverse EEA using reset `Y` as workspace;
5. recompute the discarded value into `Y`;
6. apply transcript-controlled `Z` corrections to `Y`;
7. uncompute that recomputation; and
8. swap `Y` and `A`.

The in-place multiplication schedule uses the same register reuse in this order: multiply into
`A`; measure/reset `Y`; forward EEA using `Y`; recompute into `Y`; correct phases; uncompute;
inverse EEA; swap `Y` and `A`.

The pinned Figure-14 wrapper then performs:

```text
X -= x2
Y -= ctrl*y2
Y /= X
X -= ctrl*Y^2
X += ctrl*3*x2
Y *= X
X := -X                 (if ctrl)
X += x2
Y -= ctrl*y2
```

At `n = 256`, the generator's top-level expansion contains two forward EEA calls, two inverse EEA
calls, four forward and two inverse multiply-to-zero calls, one forward and one inverse square,
512 each of `H`, X-basis measurement, reset, transcript-controlled `Z`, and register `SWAP`, plus
the listed constant arithmetic. These are generator observations, not certified Lean counts.

## Quarantined claims

The following are deliberately not available as proof premises:

1. The paper reports both `2^30.88` and `2^30.63` Toffolis for secp256k1. The displayed
   `28 * (5 * 2^16 + 70.10 million)` expression is approximately `2^30.877`, but the 70.10-million
   input is rounded. Lean will report the exact integer derived from its eventual program.
2. The supplemental repository constructs the 835-qubit controlled point-add wrapper, but it does
   not construct the complete signed-window plus semiclassical-QFT lifetime schedule. Therefore
   835 is not yet a verified whole-algorithm qubit count.
3. The supplemental Figure-14 algebra and tests require the non-exceptional domain `x1 != x2` and
   exclude an internal zero multiplier. The Lean point addition must implement the total group law,
   including identity, inverse, doubling, and exceptional cases; its final qubit bound may exceed
   835.
4. For cryptographic moduli, the supplemental function named
   `certified_uniform_steps_for_modulus` returns the paper's analytic 1,620-step value rather than
   exhaustively certifying every input. The 1,536-step secp256k1 example is one witness, not a
   worst-case proof. Phase 4 must prove the uniform 1,620-step bound independently.
5. The generator uses floating-point evaluations to choose active-window endpoints. Lean will
   replace them with exact integer/rational inequalities before window pruning is trusted.

## Frozen resource vocabulary

The paper submission counts a vector with these distinct fields:

- `x`, `h`, `cnot`, `toffoli`, and `dyadicPhase`;
- `xMeasureReset` for one X-basis measurement followed by reset;
- `classicalCorrection` for one transcript-controlled quantum correction;
- `tableLookup` until each lookup is refined to primitive gates; and
- `maxLiveQubits`, derived from concrete wire labels and proved reset-before-reuse.

Sequential counts add. Adaptive branches use their maximum operational count unless a stronger
theorem is proved. Classical bits are reported separately from logical qubits. A `toffoli` is one
final `CCX` after every multi-control decomposition; it is not silently identified with seven T
gates. Likewise, `tableLookup` may not remain opaque in a final primitive-gate resource theorem.
